Deletions

This only affects you. Each admin has their own switch.

Switched off (normal):

  • You can remove comments. A note saying you removed it stays on the ticket and in the item history.
  • Tickets and items can't be deleted. Photos and documents can still be removed (the file is kept), and items can be written off.

Switched on:

  • “Delete permanently” buttons appear on tickets, items, comments, photos and documents.
  • Deleting asks you to confirm, can't be undone, erases the files and leaves no trace in the ticket or item history.

Either way, the audit log records who removed or deleted what, and when.
